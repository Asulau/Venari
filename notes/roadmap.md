# Vitality Partners — Roadmap

Things the site copy implies we already do but we don't yet. Either build them before going public, or hold the related copy off the site until they exist.

## Per-person matching
Today: we match ONE coach to the company. The coach works with the whole team.
Future: each team member gets their own coach drawn from a vetted network.
Status: aspirational. Public copy on the site has been rewritten away from this until it's real.

## Reporting
Today: no reporting, no dashboards, no quarterly metric review.
Future: aggregate cohort metrics (adherence, mobility deltas, sleep, eNPS), board-ready PDFs, real-time renewal/utilization view.
Status: aspirational. Public copy has been rewritten to "no dashboards, by design" until reporting is built.

## HR / People dashboard
Today: not built.
Future: an HR view of cohort metrics, never individual data, with quarterly auto-generated PDFs.
Status: aspirational. Mentioned in the For Companies "Three views, one product" section. Mock UI on the live site is illustrative only.

## Group discounts / volume pricing
Today: pricing not published.
Future: tiered seat pricing (Starter / Team / Enterprise) with volume discount above 100 seats.
Status: aspirational. Public pricing has been removed from the site until the model is locked.

What you can expect

Vitality Partners is built around a coaching practice. We hold the minimum personal information needed to match members to coaches and to run the program. Individual coaching data never leaves the practice in identifiable form.

If you contact us via the booking flow, we collect what you submit (name, email, role, team size, what you want to work on). We use it only to prepare for the call and never sell, rent, or share it with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and aggregate. We do not run third-party advertising trackers, fingerprinting, or session replay.

Your rights under GDPR

If you have engaged with us and want a copy of, or deletion of, your data, write to hello@venari.se. We respond within 30 days.
